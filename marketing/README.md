# Marketing

Source for Replay's films. Each one is a deterministic HTML timeline rendered frame by frame with Playwright and encoded with ffmpeg, with an original score synthesised locally. Only the finished MP4 and poster are tracked; stills, silent masters, and audio stems are regenerated by the render scripts.

| Film | Length | Look | Story | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`promo-video-v2`](promo-video-v2/README.md) | 82 s, 30 fps | Dark, one continuous camera move | The repository's invoice demo, from recording to a supervised run | [`replay-film-1080p.mp4`](promo-video-v2/output/replay-film-1080p.mp4) |
| [`promo-video`](promo-video/README.md) | 74 s, 24 fps | Light, floating interfaces | A weekly client update, from demonstration to evidence | [`replay-promo-1080p.mp4`](promo-video/output/replay-promo-1080p.mp4) |

The v2 film is the one featured in the [project README](../README.md). Each folder has its own storyboard, accuracy guardrails, and render instructions.
