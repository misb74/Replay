# Replay promo film

## Creative idea

**A person demonstrates the judgment behind a weekly client update. Replay turns that demonstration into a reviewed, testable specification that can be exported or run safely.**

The film uses a classic product-demo grammar: one credible story, restrained copy, floating interfaces, a time jump, and a single closing thesis.

## Timeline

| Time | Story beat | Product truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–9s | A normal project board contains hidden judgment: shipped work, a moved date, and an ownerless blocker. | The hard part is the context between clicks. |
| 9–27s | The expert records and narrates the Monday client update. | Replay observes screen actions and optional narration. |
| 27–36s | Screen, actions, and narration become a draft. | Raw video remains local; Replay builds a workflow with steps, decisions, and checks. |
| 36–50s | Replay asks what counts as a launch blocker. The expert answers and confirms “save, never send.” | Ambiguity is exposed for human review; the workflow is not treated as perfect after one recording. |
| 50–55s | The exact reviewed revision is approved. | Approval is revision-bound and content-hashed. |
| 55–63s | The approved workflow becomes a playbook, browser script, or guarded runner package. | All three current export families are represented accurately. |
| 63–68s | A new week's update runs under supervision on the Mac and leaves evidence. | The runner verifies the result and does not repeat prior successful actions on retry. |
| 68–74s | Replay's positioning closes the film. | Replay is the teaching and specification layer for agents. |

## On-screen thesis

> Show the work.<br>
> Teach the agent.<br>
> Trust the result.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Do not show work continuing after the Mac closes.
- Do not imply the recording alone creates a finished agent.
- Do not imply all captured evidence stays local: selected frames, condensed actions, narration, and later run screenshots may be sent to the configured model after the user starts Build or Run.
- Keep the safety behaviour visible: ask on an unknown blocker and save the message as a draft rather than sending it.
- Show evidence and approval as outcomes, not only autonomous action.
